About

Hi, I'm Anushka.

A CPA-qualified finance leader with 20+ years' experience — 12 of them in FP&A and finance business partnering across Coles Group and Linfox Logistics. I now mentor and coach finance professionals who want to grow with the same clarity and confidence I look for in every business case.

My story

I've spent my career translating complex financial and operational data into clear, forward-looking insight for boards and executives — from leading finance business partnering for a $700m wholesale distribution model at Coles Group, to building finance functions from the ground up, to guiding large-scale transformation and ERP projects.

Along the way, the part of the job I've valued most has been the people side of it: building and developing finance teams, coaching individual contributors into commercial analysts, and helping colleagues navigate the tricky, unwritten parts of a finance career — the ones no textbook covers.

That's what led me to formalise mentoring and coaching as its own practice. Since March 2025, I've volunteered as a mentor with CPA Australia, and I now also work with private clients who want dedicated, one-to-one support as they grow.

How I work

I bring the same approach to mentoring that I bring to finance: listen closely, ask the right questions, and give practical, honest guidance — not generic advice. Sessions are collaborative, grounded in your real situation, and focused on outcomes you can act on straight away.
