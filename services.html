Services

Mentoring and coaching for every stage of a finance career

Every engagement starts with a conversation about where you are and where you want to be. Below are the ways I typically work with clients — sessions can be one-off, ongoing, or built around a specific goal.

01

1:1 Career Mentoring

Ongoing, confidential mentoring for finance professionals navigating growth, a career plateau, or a difficult decision. We meet regularly to work through what's in front of you and plan your next move.

02

FP&A & Commercial Finance Coaching

Practical coaching on budgeting, forecasting, business partnering and stakeholder influence — the skills that turn a good analyst into a trusted advisor to the business.

03

CPA / CIMA Pathway Guidance

Support for candidates working through professional qualifications, from study strategy to translating the exams into real workplace impact.

04

Leadership Development

Coaching for finance managers stepping into people leadership for the first time — building and developing a team, giving feedback, and leading through change.

05

Career Transition & Interview Coaching

Preparing for a move — internal promotion, new employer, or industry switch. We work on your story, your CV, and how you present your commercial impact.

06

Team & Group Workshops

Workshop-style sessions for finance teams on business partnering, communication, or building commercial confidence — tailored to your team's context.

How it works

A simple, low-pressure process

Step 1

Step 2

Step 3

Regular sessions with clear takeaways, so progress compounds between conversations.
